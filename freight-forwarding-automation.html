Why quoting comes first

It earns money. Shipment creation, customs and invoice checks save cost on jobs you already have. Quoting decides whether you get the job at all. A faster, complete quote is a better chance of winning it.

It repeats more than anything else. Every job starts as a quote request, and many requests never become jobs. That is a lot of reading, looking up and typing for work that pays nothing if you lose.

Speed is the whole game. Shippers ask several forwarders at once. The first complete, accurate reply has a head start, so a request that waits in the inbox is a request at risk. See freight quote response time.

It needs no system change. Quote automation can work from the email you already receive and the rate sheet you already keep. No CargoWise project, no data migration.

What good quote automation looks like

1. Reads

Any request format

Plain emails, forwarded chains, PDFs, scans and packing lists. Lane, cargo, weights, dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address pulled out.

2. Prices

From your rate sheet

Every line from your own rates by fixed rules, with your markup and minimums, and each line traced to the row it came from.

3. Flags

Never guesses

Missing lane, expired rate, DG, reefer, oversize or RoRo goes to your team, with the partner rate request already written.

4. Replies

Drafted or sent

The reply and PDF quote in your company's name, for your team to check, or sent automatically when fully priced.

Freight forwarding automation questions

What is freight forwarding automation?

Freight forwarding automation is software doing the repeat work in a forwarder's back office: reading customer emails, pricing quotes from rate sheets, requesting rates from partners, creating shipments from documents, filing customs entries, checking carrier invoices and sending status updates. Staff check the exceptions instead of typing every job.

What should a freight forwarder automate first?

Quoting. It is the only back-office task that directly decides whether you win the job, it repeats dozens of times a week, and a slow reply loses the customer. Quote replies built from your own rate sheet also need no change to your TMS or CargoWise setup.

Will automation replace my operations team?

No. Automation takes the typing and lookups. Your team still owns the rates, the customer relationships and every exception: new lanes, special cargo, disputes and anything unusual.

Do I need CargoWise or a TMS to automate quoting?

No. Quote automation that works from email and your existing rate sheet runs alongside CargoWise, Magaya or a spreadsheet, without changing them.

How is AI used in freight forwarding automation?

Mostly to read unstructured input: emails, PDFs, scans and photos of documents. The safest systems use AI only to read, and keep prices and money on fixed rules from your own data, so a figure is never invented.

How much does freight forwarding automation cost?
