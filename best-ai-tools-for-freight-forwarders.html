Quote requests

Anything the sheet doesn't cover is flagged, never guessed. Fully priced quotes can be sent automatically from your own mailbox.

2. Expedock (Freya): best if you want an AI agent plus a human operator

Freya is Expedock's AI agent for forwarders. Its published tasks include classifying email, drafting freight quotes (from an existing rate sheet or by emailing carriers and truckers for spot rates), shipment status replies, booking coordination and invoice posting, with an Expedock operator reviewing its work.

  • + Covers quoting and back-office tasks in one service
  • + Can request spot rates from carriers and truckers
  • + Human operator in the loop
  • − Pricing not published
  • − A managed service rather than a tool your team runs

Documents, customs and AP

4. Raft: best for customs entries and AP invoices

Raft's AI extracts data from commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading and certificates of origin for customs clearance, validates and matches carrier invoices for AP, and classifies and routes inbound email to the right team.

  • + Customs, finance and operations on one platform
  • + Audit trails and native email integration
  • − Quoting is not listed among its solutions
  • − Pricing not published; demo required

Price: On request.

Inside your TMS

Each includes quoting and rate management as part of the wider system. CargoWise and Magaya are full forwarding platforms; Logistaas is a lower-cost TMS for small and mid-sized forwarders.

  • + Everything in one system
  • + No extra vendor
  • − Requests still have to be keyed in from email
  • − Changing TMS is a large project

Price: Part of your licence (CargoWise transaction pricing; Logistaas from US$45–85 per user per month plus implementation, published Oct 2026). More

General AI assistants

7. ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot: best for reading and drafting everyday email

Good at summarising threads, pulling details out of messy emails and drafting replies. They don't hold your current rates, can get surcharges and arithmetic wrong without saying so, and can't show where a figure came from, so they shouldn't set prices.

  • + Cheap and already available to most teams
  • + Useful well beyond quoting
  • − No rate lookup or audit trail
  • − Check data settings before pasting customer emails

Price: Included in many Microsoft 365 and ChatGPT business plans. More

How to choose

  • Count the hours first. For most forwarders it's quoting or data entry. Start there.
  • Ask where the numbers come from. For quotes, the price should come from your rates by fixed rules, not from the model.
  • Test on your own work. Send a vendor 20 of your recent requests or documents and check the output line by line before you sign.
  • Keep your TMS. Tools that work alongside CargoWise or Magaya go live in days; a TMS change takes months.

Questions

What is the best AI tool for freight forwarders?

It depends on the job. For answering quote requests from your own rate sheet, Laneworks; for customs entries and AP invoices, Raft; for an AI agent working alongside an outsourced operator, Expedock; for rate management with carrier rates, Freightify. Most forwarders start with the task that takes the most hours each week, which is usually quoting or data entry.

Can AI quote freight accurately?

Yes, when the AI only reads the request and the price comes from your own rate sheet by fixed rules. Ask any vendor to run your own recent requests against your rates and show the totals line by line.

Do these tools replace CargoWise or Magaya?

No. Laneworks, Raft and Expedock work alongside your TMS. Replacing the TMS itself is a separate, larger project.

Can we just use ChatGPT?

It is useful for reading and drafting emails, but it doesn't hold your rates or show where a price came from, so it shouldn't set prices.
