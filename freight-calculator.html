How the numbers are worked out

  • CBM = length × width × height (cm) ÷ 1,000,000, times the number of pieces.
  • Volumetric weight (air) = length × width × height (cm) ÷ divisor, per piece. 6,000 is the usual airline cargo divisor (1 cbm = about 167 kg); many courier and express services use 5,000 (1 cbm = 200 kg).
  • Chargeable weight (air) = the greater of total actual weight and total volumetric weight, rounded up to the next half kilo. Check your airline's rounding rule and the next rate break: sometimes charging at the higher break is cheaper.
  • Revenue tonnes (LCL) = the greater of CBM and weight in tonnes (1 RT = 1 cbm or 1,000 kg), with the consolidator's minimum applied.
