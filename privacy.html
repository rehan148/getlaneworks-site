Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Laneworks is a product of Tessaro Pty Ltd (ACN 697 269 846), Melbourne, Australia. This explains what we collect, why, and how to get it removed.

Business contact details

We contact freight forwarders and customs brokers using business email addresses they publish on their own websites, such as info@ or sales@. For each one we keep the company name, the address, the page where it was published, and the dates we emailed. We use this only to tell businesses about Laneworks. We don't buy lists or guess addresses.

Emails you forward to us

If you forward quote requests for a trial or pilot, we use them only to draft your quotes. They're processed by our software and by Anthropic's AI service, which reads the email text. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models. Forwarded emails are deleted after 30 days, and your rate sheet is never shared or used for anyone else.

Where data is stored

Email is handled by Google Workspace. Our processing runs on servers we control. Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

Your choices

  • Stop our emails at any time: unsubscribe, or reply "unsubscribe". We act within 5 working days.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it: email rehan@getlaneworks.com.
  • Complaints: tell us first. In Australia you can also contact the OAIC (oaic.gov.au). In the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Legal basis (UK and EU)

For business contact details we rely on legitimate interests: telling businesses about a service relevant to their work. You can object at any time by unsubscribing.
