How a Laneworks quote is priced

The most common question forwarders ask about AI quoting is whether it makes prices up. In Laneworks it can't: the AI never sets a price. Here is exactly what happens between a customer's email arriving and a priced reply landing with your team.

1. The AI reads the request

The customer's email is read by Claude, Anthropic's AI model. Its only job is to pull out the details: origin, destination, mode, container sizes or weight and volume, Incoterm, cargo type and ready date. It doesn't look up rates and it doesn't write numbers.

2. Fixed rules price it from your rate sheet

Pricing is done by plain, fixed rules run against your own rate sheet: the freight rate for that lane and equipment, then each origin, destination and accessorial charge that applies, then your markup as you've set it. A different markup can be set for each customer (customer-specific pricing). The same request priced twice gives the same answer.

  • Every line on the draft names the rate-sheet row it came from, so any number can be checked in seconds.
  • Only rates valid on the shipping date are used. Expired rates are skipped, not quoted.
  • Currencies are kept as they are on your sheet, with no silent conversion.

3. Anything uncertain is flagged, not guessed

If something can't be priced from your sheet, the draft says so in plain words instead of filling the gap:

  • A lane, container size or charge that isn't on the sheet is named as a missing rate.
  • A detail the customer didn't give, such as the Incoterm, is marked as an assumption for your team to confirm.
  • An attachment that couldn't be read is called out, so nothing is quoted from half the request.

For anything missing from your sheet, the draft also includes the rate request to your carrier or agent, ready to send, and the quote is priced automatically when their rate comes back. How custom quotes work

4. Your team decides what goes out

By default the priced reply comes to your team as a draft, and you send it from your own mailbox. Teams that want faster replies can switch on auto-reply, which only sends when every line is priced from the sheet, nothing is flagged and the total is under a limit they set. Everything else still comes to the team as a draft, and a copy of every sent reply comes back to you.
