Refer a forwarder, earn 20%

If you work with freight forwarders or customs brokers (consulting, software, accounting, recruiting, a network), you can refer them to Laneworks and earn 20% of each referred customer's first-year revenue. On the standard plan that is about A$6,000 per customer, paid monthly as they pay us.

How it works

  • Your client forwards one real quote request to try@getlaneworks.com and mentions you, or you email rehan@getlaneworks.com with their name first. Either way, the referral is recorded against you before anything is paid.
  • They see their own drafted quote within minutes. No call, no integration; it works by email alongside CargoWise or any other system.
  • If they become a customer, you receive 20% of what they pay in the first 12 months, paid monthly within 14 days of our receiving each payment. No cap, no minimum.
  • Your client never pays more because they came through you; the founding and pilot prices are the same.

What to send them

One line is enough: "Forward one of your quote-request emails to try@getlaneworks.com and see what comes back. Takes two minutes." If you'd like a longer note or a demo on their own emails first, email us.

Terms

Referral commission is 20% of net fees (ex GST) actually received from the referred customer in their first 12 months, for referrals made in writing before the customer's first payment. Paid to an Australian or overseas bank account by transfer; GST is added for Australian GST-registered partners. Laneworks may end the programme for new referrals with 30 days' notice; commissions on existing referrals continue. Governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
