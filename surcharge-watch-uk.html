Surcharge Watch UK: charge changes for Dec 2026 and Jan 2027

The carrier surcharges, UK terminal charges and HMRC changes that touch UK rate sheets and declarations over the next ten weeks. Every line links to its source notice so you can check it yourself.

The short version: Asia to North Europe peak season surcharges start on 1 December, DP World's import energy charge is already up, the EU ETS surcharge resets on 1 January, CDS changes go live on 26 November and UK CBAM starts on 1 January.

HMRC and border changes

  • CDS release, 26 November. New document codes Y388 and Y501 and new AI codes GMSCK and GRDEF (GRDEF from 30 December). Pre-lodged declarations for affected goods arriving on or after 26 November must be amended or they'll be rejected. Source: CDS release guidance for traders (published via BIFA).
  • UK CBAM from 1 January 2027. Aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen, iron and steel. Importers register once they pass GBP 50,000 of CBAM goods; the first return and payment are due 31 May 2028. Source: GOV.UK.
  • Fruit and veg easement extended. Medium-risk fruit and vegetables from the EU, Switzerland and Liechtenstein stay exempt from GB plant health checks until 31 January 2028. Source: Defra Plant Health Portal.
  • Common User Charge at Sevington and Dover: unchanged. GBP 10 or GBP 29 per commodity line, capped at 5 lines per CHED. Source: GOV.UK.
