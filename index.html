What forwarders ask first.

Will it reply to my customers automatically?

If you want it to. Quotes fully priced from your rates go straight to the customer from your own mailbox. Anything unusual (a missing rate, a guessed detail, a quote above your limit) comes to your team as a draft.

Does it work with CargoWise?

Yes. It works from email, so it runs alongside CargoWise or any system without touching it.

What if it gets a price wrong?

Prices come from your rate sheet by fixed rules, never from the AI. A missing lane or charge is flagged, never guessed.

How long does setup take?

About an hour on your side: one forwarding rule and your rate sheet. We load it and check the first replies with you.

Our rates change every week. Do we keep a spreadsheet updated?

No. Forward the carrier's rate notice or your new sheet. Rates update in minutes and you get one email listing every change. Reply "undo" to roll it back.

Which freight does it quote?

FCL, LCL and air, origin and destination charges by Incoterm, door delivery by postcode zone and customs clearance. It reads PDF, Excel and Word attachments too.

Who sees our data?

Only Laneworks, to price your quotes. Forwarded emails are deleted after 30 days. Data & security

What does it cost?

The trial is free. After that it's a monthly fee per quoting mailbox, with unlimited quotes and users, no per-quote charges and no contract. If it isn't saving your team 5 hours a week by day 30, month two is free. Pricing

What do we get besides the replies?

A PDF quotation in your name with every reply, your website's quote form answered in about two minutes, a daily list of quotes nobody has answered with the follow-up already written, rate updates by email, and a weekly report of replies sent, response times and jobs won.
