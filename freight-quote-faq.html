Freight quote FAQ

Freight quote FAQ: short answers to the questions forwarders and customs brokers ask most about quoting, with links to the full guides.

How do freight forwarders calculate a quote?

A forwarder reads the request (lane, cargo, Incoterm, delivery address), takes the buy rate for the main freight leg, adds its margin, then adds the origin and destination charges the Incoterm puts on the customer, plus delivery and clearance if asked.

What is an all-in freight rate?

An all-in rate is a single price that bundles the base ocean or air freight with surcharges such as BAF, PSS and sometimes origin charges, instead of listing each one.

What is a spot rate in freight?

A spot rate is a one-off price for a single shipment at current market rates, as opposed to a contract rate agreed for a period. Spot quotes are short-lived, often valid for days, not weeks.

How should I respond to a freight quote request?

Reply fast, confirm what you understood (lane, cargo, Incoterm, dates), price every charge on its own line with a total, state validity and exclusions, and ask for anything missing instead of guessing.

How can freight forwarders win more quotes?

Reply first, make the quote easy to accept, and follow up. Customers usually ask several forwarders, and a complete, clear quote that arrives first is the one they act on.

What is a freight rate sheet?

A freight rate sheet (or tariff) is the forwarder's table of prices: freight rates by lane and equipment, plus origin, destination, delivery and clearance charges, with currencies and validity dates.

Can AI reply to freight quote emails?

Yes. AI can read a customer's quote-request email and attachments reliably. The safe way to use it is to let the AI read the request and let fixed rules price it from your own rate sheet, so every number is traceable.

What is included in a door-to-door freight quote?

A door-to-door quote covers pickup at origin, origin charges and export clearance, the main freight, destination charges, import clearance and delivery to the consignee's address.

How long should it take to reply to a freight quote request?

As fast as you can make it complete. Customers comparing forwarders often book the first clear, complete quote, so same-hour replies win more jobs than next-day ones. See how to get quote replies under an hour.

What should a freight quote include?

The shipment details you priced on, every charge line grouped by origin, freight and destination, the Incoterm, the total and currency, whether tax is included, validity, and exclusions. The quote reply template shows the layout.

What information do I need from the customer to quote freight?

Origin and destination, cargo description, number of pieces with weights and dimensions, Incoterm, cargo-ready date, and the delivery address if door delivery is needed. For dangerous goods, also the UN number and SDS.

How is air freight chargeable weight calculated?

It's the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight; volumetric weight is length × width × height in cm ÷ 6,000 for most airline cargo. Try the free freight calculator.

How is LCL freight charged?

Per revenue tonne (W/M): the greater of cubic metres and tonnes, usually with a minimum of 1 revenue tonne. See how to calculate an LCL quote.

How long should a freight quote be valid?

No longer than the buy rates behind it: often to the end of the carrier's rate period for sea freight, and a week or less for air. See freight quote validity.

What are BAF, PSS and THC on a freight quote?

BAF covers fuel cost changes, PSS is a peak season surcharge, and THC is the terminal handling charge at origin or destination. See ocean freight surcharges explained.

How do freight forwarders set margins on quotes?

Most add a markup per line or on the total, with a dollar floor per shipment so small jobs still pay. See how to set margins on freight quotes.

Can freight quotes be automated?

Yes. The slow part is reading the email and assembling the reply; that can be automated while prices still come from your own rate sheet and a person checks before sending. See freight quote email automation.
