How it works

1 · Forward

Send us the request as it is

Forward the customer's email, attachments and all. Packing lists and invoices in PDF, Excel, CSV or Word are read too.

2 · Draft

We read it and price it

The AI pulls out the lane, cargo, dimensions and Incoterm. Fixed rules then price it from your rate sheet: no AI-guessed prices.

3 · Send

Sent, or checked first

With auto-reply on, a fully priced quote goes straight to your customer from your mailbox. Otherwise you get a ready-to-send reply, what we read from the email, and the price breakdown. If something's missing, the draft asks the customer for it.

Built for forwarders who quote by email

Most small forwarders and customs brokers don't quote in a portal. Requests arrive by email, in every format, and someone on the team rebuilds each one in a spreadsheet. That's the step Laneworks takes over.

  • Your rates, not ours. Pricing comes from your own rate sheet, with your margins and charges. Change a rate by sending the updated sheet.
  • Works alongside CargoWise. Nothing to integrate: it never connects to your mailbox, TMS or CargoWise.
  • Every line is traceable. Each charge names the rate-sheet row it came from, so a check takes seconds.
  • Auto-reply only when it's safe. Missing rate, guessed detail or a big quote? It comes to you as a draft instead.
  • Air, FCL and LCL. Including chargeable weight and W/M. Try the free freight calculator.

Questions

Do we need to change our TMS or CargoWise setup?

No. It works from email. It never connects to your mailbox, your TMS or CargoWise.

Where do the prices come from?

From your own rate sheet. The AI reads the email; pricing is done by fixed rules against your rates, and every line names the row it came from.

What if a lane or charge isn't in our rate sheet?

The draft says which one is missing instead of guessing, so nothing goes out on a made-up number.

Does Laneworks email our customers?

Never. The draft goes to the people you nominate, and you send it from your own mailbox.

What happens to our data?

Forwarded emails are deleted within 30 days; your rates are used only for your quotes. The detail is on the data and security page.
