What one month looked like

SAMPLE

A sample month of 7 closed jobs (34 sell and cost lines) and 10 carrier invoice lines:

Unbilled charges (paid out, never billed on), 6 linesAUD 1,435 + USD 420
Sold below cost, 1 lineAUD 145
Carrier invoice lines charged twice, 2 linesAUD 1,935
Total foundAUD 3,515 + USD 420

Largest items: freight invoiced twice on one job (AUD 1,850), demurrage paid and never billed (AUD 650), detention paid and never billed (USD 420). Lines that can't be matched with confidence are listed under "Needs a look" and kept out of the totals.

What to send

  • Closed jobs, one month, as an Excel or CSV export with one row per charge line: job number, charge, sell, cost and currency. Any column order works.
  • Carrier invoices for the same jobs (optional), to catch charges billed twice.
  • Your agreed carrier rates (optional), to catch invoices above what you agreed.

What comes back

  • A short summary by email: what was found, in money, per currency.
  • A one-page report.
  • A spreadsheet with every flagged line, the reason, and the row it came from.

Nothing is estimated. Tax lines are left out, and anything that can't be matched with confidence is flagged for a look, not counted.

Your data

Files you send are used only to run your check. Ask for them to be deleted at any time: hello@getlaneworks.com. More on our security page.

Then what

Laneworks is the back office for freight forwarders: every quote priced from your own rates, and the charges your jobs miss caught before they close. If the check finds money, the next step is a 20-minute call about running it on every job.
