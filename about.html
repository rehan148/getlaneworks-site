About Laneworks

Laneworks answers freight forwarders' quote requests. A customer's email is read, every line is priced from the forwarder's own rate sheet by fixed rules, and the reply is written in the forwarder's format, usually within two minutes. It works by email alongside CargoWise, Magaya or any other forwarding system, so there is nothing to install or migrate.

Who it's for

Independent forwarders, NVOCCs and customs brokers who answer FCL, LCL and air quote requests by email, in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Singapore, the US and Canada.

How accuracy is handled

  • The AI reads requests and writes replies. It never sets a price.
  • Every price comes from the forwarder's own rate sheet, and every line shows the row it came from.
  • Anything that can't be priced from the sheet becomes a draft for the team, with the gap marked.
  • Replies go straight to customers only when the forwarder switches that on, and only for fully priced quotes.

Data handling is described on our security page and in our privacy policy.

Editorial standards

Articles, guides and tools on this site are written by the Laneworks team for people who price freight every day. Calculations follow standard industry conventions (for example W/M for LCL and the 6,000 divisor for airline chargeable weight), competitor prices are taken from the vendor's own published pages and dated, and articles show their publication date. If you spot an error, email hello@getlaneworks.com and we will correct it.
