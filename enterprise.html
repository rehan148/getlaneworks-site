What's included

  • Tenders priced in hours. Forward a shipper's tender spreadsheet and every lane comes back priced from your rates, in the shipper's own workbook: freight, origin and destination charges, total, validity and the rate each price came from. Lanes your rates don't cover are marked, with the rate request to your agents written and ready to send, and the annual value of the tender at the stated volumes.
  • No quote below your own rates. Every line is priced from your current rates with every charge on your sheet, so missed surcharges and expired rates stop leaking margin. Our free margin check shows what it's been costing you: send 50 quotes your team sent last month with the rates used, and we show every line priced below your rates or missing a charge.
  • Every quote inbox, every branch. Requests by email and website form are read, priced line by line from your rates and drafted in your company's name with a PDF quotation, usually within minutes, day and night.
  • Custom quotes, chased. Lanes, DG and charges your rates don't cover get rate requests to your own carriers and agents, written and ready to send, and are priced automatically when the rate comes back.
  • Auto-reply where you choose. Requests priced entirely from your rates, with nothing flagged and under the limits you set, can go straight to the customer from your own mailbox. Everything else comes to your team to check.
  • Follow-up and win tracking. Every morning your team gets the quotes nobody has answered, with the follow-up already written and Won / Lost buttons, so win rate is measured, not guessed.
  • Your rates kept current for you. Carrier notices and new sheets are applied by email with a summary of every change; our team reviews your rates every week and flags expiring lanes.
  • A named account manager and a monthly results review: quotes priced, minutes to reply, after-hours requests caught, busiest lanes, jobs won and win rate, broken down by branch and customer.

What it's worth

An example, using your own numbers in the demo: a team handling 2,000 quote requests a month at 20 minutes each spends about 670 hours a month quoting. At a fully loaded A$45 an hour, that's around A$30,000 a month. A 200-lane tender that takes a pricing analyst three days is priced the same afternoon. And every under-priced line recovered goes straight to margin. Laneworks Enterprise is priced well below what it gives back.

How the pilot works

  1. Week 0, setup. We load your rates (Excel sheets or exports from your rate system), your agents and your quote templates, and connect one branch or desk.
  2. Weeks 1 to 4, live. Real quote requests are priced and drafted for your team. Nothing reaches a customer unless your team sends it.
  3. Agreed targets, measured. Before we start, we agree what success means: for example the share of standard requests drafted ready to send, and minutes from request to draft. We report against them every week.
  4. Decision. At day 30 you see the results and decide. The pilot fee is credited against setup if you continue.

Questions

Who is Enterprise for?

Forwarders with several branches or a dedicated pricing team, typically handling 500 or more quote requests a month across air, sea and road.

Do we need to change our TMS or CargoWise?

No. Laneworks works by email next to CargoWise or any other system. Nothing to install or migrate.

Does it make up prices?

No. The AI reads the request; fixed rules price every line from your own rates, and anything that can't be priced is flagged for your team. Every line names the rate it came from.

How long does setup take?

Usually under a week for the first branch, depending on how your rates are kept.

Can we start with one branch?

Yes. Most pilots start with one branch or desk, then extend to the rest.
