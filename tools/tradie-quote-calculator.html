Where trade quotes leak money

  • No minimum charge. A 30-minute job at $110 an hour is $55 of labour. With a $250 minimum, it's $250.
  • Materials at cost. A 20% markup on $85 of parts is $17 more per job.
  • After-hours priced like a Tuesday. 2 hours at $110 with a 50% loading is $110 more.
  • Slow quotes. The customer usually books whoever sends a clear price first.

Questions

How do tradies price a small job?

Most charge an hourly rate per person, plus a call-out fee, with a minimum charge so short jobs still pay. Materials are marked up on cost, after-hours work carries a loading, and GST goes on top.

What is a minimum charge?

The least you bill for any job, whatever the time on site. With a $250 minimum, a 30-minute job at $110 an hour is $250, not $55 plus call-out.

Should I mark up materials?

Many trades do, to cover the time spent sourcing, collecting and warrantying parts. The calculator lets you set your own markup, including zero.
