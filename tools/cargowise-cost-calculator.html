Questions

How much does CargoWise cost per shipment?

At WiseTech's published list prices (version 26.10), the Forwarding Value Pack is US$19.95 per FCL import, US$13.30 per LCL or air import, US$12.75 per FCL export, US$8.35 per LCL or air export, and US$3.95 or US$2.63 for gateway, transit and co-load shipments.

Are CargoWise customs entries charged separately?

Customs procedures are in the Customs Value Pack at US$3.28 per export or core procedure and US$9.95 per import procedure. Stand-alone customs is included in the Forwarding Value Pack.

Do payment terms change the CargoWise price?

Yes. The published list adds 2% for net 30, 4% for net 45 and 6% for net 60 terms; net 14 has no surcharge.

Is this my actual CargoWise bill?

It is an estimate at list price. Contract prices, transitional pricing protection for customers on the Special Offer, hosting and other optional services can change the real figure.
