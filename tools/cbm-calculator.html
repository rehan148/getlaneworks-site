How CBM is worked out

  • CBM per carton = length × width × height (cm) ÷ 1,000,000.
  • Total CBM = CBM per carton × number of cartons.
  • Cubic feet = CBM × 35.3147.
  • LCL revenue tonnes = the greater of CBM and weight in tonnes (1 RT = 1 cbm or 1,000 kg).
  • Container fill uses typical usable volumes, not the full internal volume: real loads leave gaps.

Questions

How do you calculate CBM?

Multiply length × width × height of one carton in metres to get its cubic metres, then multiply by the number of cartons. In centimetres, multiply the three sides and divide by 1,000,000. Example: a 60 × 40 × 50 cm carton is 0.12 cbm, so 40 of them are 4.8 cbm.

How do I convert inches to CBM?

Multiply length × width × height in inches to get cubic inches, then divide by 61,024. Or multiply cubic feet by 0.0283. One cubic metre is about 35.3 cubic feet.

How many CBM fit in a 20ft or 40ft container?

A 20ft container holds about 33 cbm inside, but about 25–28 cbm is a realistic load once you allow for carton sizes and gaps. A 40ft holds about 67 cbm (55–58 realistic) and a 40ft high cube about 76 cbm (65–68 realistic). Use the container capacity calculator to count cartons by size.

When is a shipment worth a full container instead of LCL?

As a rough rule, from about 13–15 cbm a 20ft is often cheaper than LCL, but it depends on your rates, local charges and the destination. Compare both quotes; a forwarder can price both from the same request.
