Questions

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit as a percentage of cost; margin is profit as a percentage of the sell price. On a cost of 1,000 sold at 1,200, the markup is 20% but the margin is 16.7%. A 20% margin needs a 25% markup.

What margin do freight forwarders make?

It varies widely by mode, lane, customer and size of forwarder. Gross margins on freight are commonly quoted in the 10–25% range, with higher margins on value-added services like customs clearance and delivery. Know your own numbers per customer.

Should I mark up every line of a quote?

Many forwarders apply different markups to ocean or air freight, local charges and clearance, and fixed fees to some lines. Laneworks applies your own rules per customer, so the same request is priced the same way every time.

How do I convert margin to markup?

Markup = margin ÷ (1 − margin). For a 15% margin: 0.15 ÷ 0.85 = 17.6% markup. Margin = markup ÷ (1 + markup).
