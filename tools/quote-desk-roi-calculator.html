How to read it

Hours given back is time your team no longer spends building routine quotes. Most teams spend it on the requests that need judgement, on following up quotes that went quiet, and on answering faster during peaks.

Value per month is that time at your staff cost, plus gross profit from any extra jobs if you enter a win-rate gain. Return on plan divides it by the monthly price.

The numbers that matter most are the share drafted ready to send and the minutes per quote. Both can be measured on your own requests before you pay anything: send 20 recent quote requests and the rate sheet used, and we send back what Laneworks would have quoted or flagged for each. Questions

How is the time saving calculated?

Quote requests per month, times the share drafted ready to send, times the minutes saved on each (minutes by hand minus minutes to check a draft). Requests that are flagged still go to your team and are counted as no saving, so the figure is conservative.

Where does the 64% ready-to-send figure come from?

From the public Laneworks benchmark: of 22 typical quote requests run against a sample rate sheet, 14 were priced exactly (64%). The rest were flagged or answered with questions. Your own share depends on how complete your rate sheet is; the free accuracy test on 20 of your own requests measures it.

Does replying faster win more jobs?

Often, but by how much varies by trade lane and customer, so the calculator leaves it at zero. Enter your own estimate in percentage points if you want to include it.

A flat monthly price per quoting mailbox with unlimited quotes and users: A$750 a month for the first six months on the introductory rate, then A$1,500 locked in; Standard A$2,500; Enterprise from A$5,000 for multi-branch teams. Prices exclude GST.
