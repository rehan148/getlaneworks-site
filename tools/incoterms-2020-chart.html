Questions

Which Incoterms rules are for sea freight only?

FAS, FOB, CFR and CIF are for sea and inland waterway transport only. EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU and DDP can be used for any mode, including containers, where FCA, CPT and CIP are usually the better fit than FOB, CFR and CIF.

What changed in Incoterms 2020?

DAT was renamed DPU (delivered at place unloaded, not only a terminal); CIP now requires all-risks insurance (Institute Cargo Clauses A) while CIF stays at minimum cover (Clauses C); FCA can require an on-board bill of lading; and security costs are allocated explicitly.

Who pays destination terminal handling charges under CIF?

The buyer, unless the seller's contract of carriage includes them. In practice, many CIF and CFR shipments arrive with destination charges for the buyer, which is why importers ask forwarders to quote them.

What does a forwarder quote under FOB?

For an importer buying FOB, the forwarder quotes from the origin port onwards: ocean freight, destination terminal and documentation charges, customs clearance and delivery to the door if asked.
