Where removals quotes leak money

  • The minimum charge. A 2-hour job billed at 2 hours when your minimum is 3 loses an hour every time.
  • Stairs and heavy items found out on the day, not asked for on the enquiry.
  • Weekend rates applied by one person and not by another.
  • Slow replies. The customer usually books whoever sends a clear price first.

Questions

How do removalists price a local move?

Most charge an hourly rate that depends on crew size, with a minimum number of hours and a call-out fee for travel to and from the depot. Stairs, pianos and other heavy items, packing materials and weekend or public holiday work are added on top, then GST.

What is a minimum charge on a removals quote?

The fewest hours billed on any job. If the minimum is 3 hours and the job takes 2, the customer pays for 3. Forget it on a short job and that hour is given away.
