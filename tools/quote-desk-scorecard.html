Questions

Who is the scorecard for?

Freight forwarders, customs brokers and carriers that price quote requests from a rate sheet: owners, heads of operations and pricing managers.

Do you store my answers?

No. The score is worked out in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

What does a low score mean?

Quotes are leaking: slower replies, missed charges or quotes nobody chases. Each gap comes with the fix, and most fixes cost nothing.

Can you check our real numbers?

Yes. Send 5 recent quote requests and the rate sheet you priced them from, and we'll send back a one-page proof report in your name.
