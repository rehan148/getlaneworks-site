Questions


What should a freight quote email include?
The mode and route, the cargo (containers or weight and volume), the Incoterm, every charge line with its currency, the total, how long the quote is valid, the estimated transit time, and what is excluded, such as duty, taxes, storage and detention.
How long should a freight quote be valid?
Most forwarders quote ocean freight for 7 to 14 days, or to the end of the month for contract rates, and air freight for a shorter period because airline rates move faster. Always state an end date, not just a number of days.
