Surcharge Watch: Australian charge changes for Dec 2026 and Jan 2027

The carrier surcharges and terminal charges that change on Australian rate sheets over the next ten weeks, in one place. Every line below is taken from the carrier's or terminal's own notice, linked so you can check it yourself.

The short version: peak season surcharges from Asia are already live, ANL adds a rate restoration from 1 December plus a Fremantle congestion surcharge, and Patrick's landside charges rise on 1 January (Fremantle +38%), with a new energy charge on every container.

Biosecurity: BMSB season 2026-27

Seasonal measures apply to target goods shipped from target risk countries between 1 September 2026 and 30 April 2027. Ethyl formate is now an approved treatment (offshore providers must be under AusTreat), and brokers report ethyl-formate-treated consignments with the code TREATEDEF. The Rolled Goods Policy has been removed. Source: DAFF Industry Advice Notice 151-2026.
