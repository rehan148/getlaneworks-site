Questions

What is a landside charge?

A per-container charge that a stevedore bills to the trucking company collecting or delivering a container at the terminal, on top of the shipping line's own charges. Patrick's notice says these charges are paid first by transport operators and then recovered from shippers, which is why they end up on a forwarder's import quote.

Are Patrick's 1 January 2027 figures final?

Not yet. They come from Patrick's notice of intention dated 2 November 2026. Patrick says a final notice will be published on its website on 2 December 2026. This index will be updated when it is.

What does the 40' import total include?

Only the per-container charges listed in each table: for Patrick, the import landside charge, the electronic VBS booking fee and the energy charge; for DP World UK, ISPS, EAM and ETC. It leaves out the shipping line's terminal handling and delivery order fees, storage, cartage, customs and GST or VAT.

Why are DP World Australia, Hutchison, VICT and Felixstowe not listed?

This index uses published notices only. We could not confirm their charges for this month and for 1 January on the operator's or carrier's own pages on 24 November, so they are left out rather than estimated. They are added once confirmed.
