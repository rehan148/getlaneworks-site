Australia: terminal by terminal

Patrick, DP World, Hutchison Ports and VICT have all published their import charges from January 2027 for Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Fremantle. Patrick and DP World issued notices of intention on 2 November 2026, with final notices due on 1 December (DP World) and 2 December (Patrick). VICT and Hutchison Ports issued 60-day notices on 2 and 4 November. Check the final notices before locking in January quotes.

The headline charge is each terminal's main charge on a full import container: Patrick's landside charge, DP World's terminal access charge, VICT's infrastructure surcharge and Hutchison's infrastructure levy. Shaded rows are energy charges; DP World's applies per import or export unit by road and rail. Booking (VBS) and other fees are charged on top and differ by terminal; they are in each linked notice. All amounts AUD, ex GST.

Questions

What is a landside charge?

A per-container charge that a stevedore bills to the trucking company collecting or delivering a container at the terminal, on top of the shipping line's own charges. Patrick's notice says these charges are paid first by transport operators and then recovered from shippers, which is why they end up on a forwarder's import quote.

Are Patrick's 1 January 2027 figures final?

Not yet. They come from Patrick's notice of intention dated 2 November 2026. Patrick says a final notice will be published on its website on 2 December 2026. This index will be updated when it is.

What does the 40' import total include?

Only the per-container charges listed in each table: for Patrick, the import landside charge, the electronic VBS booking fee and the energy charge; for DP World UK, ISPS, EAM and ETC. It leaves out the shipping line's terminal handling and delivery order fees, storage, cartage, customs and GST or VAT.

Are DP World, Hutchison Ports and VICT's January figures final?

Not yet. DP World's notices of intention of 2 November 2026 say final notices will be published on its customer portal by 1 December 2026. Hutchison Ports and VICT issued 60-day notices on 4 and 2 November 2026. This index will be updated if any figure changes.

Which terminals are covered?

In Australia: Patrick, DP World, Hutchison Ports and VICT at Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Fremantle, from their published notices. In the UK: DP World London Gateway and Southampton. Felixstowe is not listed: we could not confirm its charges on the operator's or a carrier's own pages, so it is left out rather than estimated.
