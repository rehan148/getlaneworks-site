Data & security

Plain answers to the questions forwarders ask before they forward a customer's email to us. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What we receive

Only what you forward: the customer's quote request and, if you choose, your rate sheet. We never connect to your mailbox, your TMS or CargoWise. Nothing is sent to your customer by us, ever; the draft goes to the people you nominate and you send it from your own mailbox.

What happens to it

  • The email text is read by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, through Anthropic's commercial API. Under those terms Anthropic does not use the data to train its models.
  • Pricing is not done by the AI. It is done by fixed rules against your rate sheet, and every line on a draft names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Email is carried by Google Workspace. Our own processing runs on systems we control in Australia. Google and Anthropic may store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

Retention

  • Forwarded email content is deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Your rate sheet is kept while you are a customer and deleted within 30 days after the agreement ends, or returned on request.
  • We keep anonymised counts and timings (how many quotes, how long each took) to run the service and the pilot report; never the content or the rates.

Who can see it

Rehan, the founder, and the systems above. Your rates are used to price your quotes and nothing else; they are never used to price for anyone else or shared with anyone.

Certifications

We are an early-stage company and do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification yet. If your procurement needs a questionnaire answered, send it and we will answer every question in writing.

Law

Personal information is handled under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Full policy: privacy. Questions or a deletion request: rehan@getlaneworks.com.
