Founding Customer Pilot Agreement

Between Tessaro Pty Ltd (ACN 697 269 846, ABN 18 697 269 846) trading as Laneworks ("Laneworks") and the customer named in the acceptance email ("Customer").

How to accept

Reply "I agree" to the email this was sent with, from a company email address, naming your company's legal name and ABN (or company number). Paying through the Laneworks payment link also confirms acceptance. No signature needed.

  1. Service. Laneworks reads quote-request emails that Customer forwards to its Laneworks address, extracts the shipment details, prices them using only the rate sheet Customer provides, and sends draft replies to Customer's nominated staff. Laneworks never contacts Customer's customers.
  2. Term. Starts on the date Customer accepts. Either party may end it at any time with 7 days' written notice (email is enough).
  3. Founding price. A$750 plus GST per quoting mailbox per month for the first 6 months, then A$1,500 plus GST per quoting mailbox per month for as long as Customer stays subscribed, locked in (the standard price is A$2,500). Billed monthly in advance by card or direct debit through Laneworks' Stripe payment link, which issues the tax invoice. If Customer asks to pay by bank transfer instead, invoices are payable within 14 days. Customer may cancel at any time under clause 2.
  4. Guarantee. If Customer's team has not saved at least 5 hours a week by day 30, measured by drafts used multiplied by an agreed time per quote, the second month is free.
  5. Case study. In return for the founding price, Customer agrees that Laneworks may publish a short case study naming Customer, with results such as minutes per quote and quotes handled. Customer approves the wording before it is published.
  6. Customer responsibilities. Customer provides an accurate rate sheet and keeps it current, chooses which emails to forward, and reviews every draft before sending. Customer is responsible for the quotes it sends.
  7. No reliance on drafts. Drafts are suggestions. Laneworks does not guarantee that any draft is complete or correct and is not liable for prices or terms in a quote Customer chooses to send.
  8. Confidentiality and data. Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. Laneworks uses forwarded emails and rate sheets only to provide the service to Customer, never to price for anyone else, deletes forwarded email content within 30 days of receipt and all Customer data within 30 days after this agreement ends (unless Customer asks for it to be returned), handles personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and its privacy policy at getlaneworks.com/privacy.html, and will tell Customer promptly about any data breach affecting Customer's data.
  9. Liability. To the extent the law allows, each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing excludes rights under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded.
  10. Intellectual property. Customer owns its rates, emails and quotes. Laneworks owns its software and may use anonymised, aggregated usage statistics (counts and timings only, never content or rates) to improve the service.
  11. General. Governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. This document and the acceptance email are the whole agreement.
