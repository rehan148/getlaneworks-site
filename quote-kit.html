Questions

Do I need new software?

No. Quote Kit runs in Excel, which most businesses already have. Pro adds a page on your own website.

What if my pricing is complicated?

Send it anyway. Zones, minimums, tiers, add-ons and seasonal surcharges are all normal. If something can't be priced by fixed rules, we'll say so before you pay anything.

What happens to my price list and quotes?

They're used only to build and check your kit, kept only while we work together, and deleted within 30 days after, or sooner if you ask.

Can it answer quote emails for me?

For freight and logistics businesses, yes: that's Laneworks, which reads quote requests and drafts the priced reply in your name.
