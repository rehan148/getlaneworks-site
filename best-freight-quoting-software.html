How to choose

  • Where do requests arrive? If it's email, a tool that reads email saves the re-keying.
  • Whose rates? Your own rate sheet, or carrier rates you search? Small forwarders mostly quote from their own sheet.
  • How is it priced? Per seat, per transaction or flat? Per-transaction pricing makes every quote cost money, including the ones you lose.
  • How long to go live? Same day for an email tool; weeks for a platform or TMS change.
