Freight rate sheet checklist: 15 checks before you quote

Most wrong or slow quotes trace back to the rate sheet: an expired rate, a missing container size, a local charge nobody listed. Run your sheet against these 15 checks once a month and quoting gets faster and safer.

Download the printable PDF

Validity

  • 1. Every rate has a 'valid to' dateNo date means nobody knows whether it's current, so every quote starts with a check.
  • 2. Expired rates are removed or markedOne expired line quoted in a busy week costs more than the time it takes to clear them out.
  • 3. Future rates carry a 'valid from' dateA GRI that starts on the 15th should be quoted from the 15th, not the day the notice arrived.

Coverage

  • 4. Every container size you sell is priced20GP, 40GP and 40HC on every FCL lane, so a 40HC request isn't priced off a 40GP rate.
  • 5. LCL has a rate per W/M and a minimumWithout the minimum, small shipments get quoted below cost.
  • 6. Air has weight breaks and a minimumMin, -45, +45, +100, +300, +500 and +1000 kg, plus the volumetric divisor you use.
  • 7. Your top 20 lanes are coveredPull last quarter's quote requests and check the lanes customers actually ask for.

Charges

  • 8. Origin and destination charges are listed per container or per shipmentTHC, documentation, ISPS, and the rest: one line each, not buried in the freight.
  • 9. Clearance, biosecurity and permits have their own linesThese are where quotes most often go out incomplete.
  • 10. Delivery is priced by zone or postcodeA flat 'metro delivery' figure undercharges the far suburbs and overcharges the near ones.
  • 11. Surcharges are named, not mergedBAF, PSS, peak and fuel surcharges change separately from base freight.

Quoting rules

  • 12. Each rate's currency is statedUSD freight and local-currency charges on the same quote, with no silent conversions.
  • 13. Your markup or margin is written downPer mode or per customer, so two people quoting the same request get the same price.
  • 14. Exclusions are standardDuty, taxes, storage, detention and demurrage: the same wording on every quote.
  • 15. One person owns the sheetCarrier notices go to one place and get merged the same day.
