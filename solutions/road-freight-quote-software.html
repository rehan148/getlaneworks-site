Built for how transport quotes really arrive

  • Messy emails. "Need a price on 2 plts, about 800kg, Laverton to Wagga, tailgate at delivery." Laneworks reads it the way your ops team would.
  • Suburbs and postcodes. Lanes can be matched by town or postcode range, so deliveries land in the right zone.
  • Fuel and extras. Fuel levies are added as a percentage. Tail-lift and residential delivery are charged when the customer asks for them, and offered in the notes when they don't.
  • Gaps are asked, not guessed. No pallet count or weight? The reply asks for it. No rate for the lane? Your team gets it flagged.
  • A proper quotation. Each reply carries a PDF quote in your company's name.

Questions

What road jobs can Laneworks price?

Pallet rates, per-kg rates charged on the greater of actual or cubic weight, full truck loads, container cartage from the wharf by delivery zone, minimum charges, fuel levies and accessorials such as tail-lift and residential delivery when they're on your rate card.

Can it match suburbs and postcodes to my zones?

Yes. Lanes can be set by town or by postcode range, so a delivery to a suburb finds the right zone.

What happens when the customer leaves out pallets or weights?

Laneworks asks the one question needed to price it, such as how many pallets and roughly what weight, instead of guessing.

What if the lane isn't on my rate card?

It isn't priced. The job comes to your team flagged, with what's missing, so nobody quotes a made-up rate.

Do I need a TMS?

No. Laneworks works from email and the rate card you already keep in a spreadsheet.
