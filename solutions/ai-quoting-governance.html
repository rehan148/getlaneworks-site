What gets flagged instead of guessed

  • A lane, container size or charge that isn't on your rate sheet, or a rate that has expired.
  • Reefer and dangerous goods without a matching rate.
  • Missing details the price depends on (weight, volume, Incoterm, delivery postcode): the draft asks the customer instead.
  • Anything that isn't a quote request, such as a status chaser or a newsletter, is left alone.

Where the data goes

Email is received by Microsoft 365 and drafted replies are sent through Resend; the email text is read by Anthropic's model; Laneworks' own processing runs on systems in Australia. Microsoft, Resend and Anthropic may store data outside Australia, including in the United States. Rate sheets are used only to price that forwarder's quotes and are never shared. Questions

Is AI quoting allowed under IFCBAA's position?

IFCBAA's position paper sets no position on quoting. Its principles (human review, auditability, no AI decisions with compliance consequences, protected client data) are the ones Laneworks is built around.

Who is accountable for a quote Laneworks drafts?

Your business, as with any quote your team sends. That is why drafts go to your team by default and every line shows the rate-sheet row behind it.

Does Laneworks train AI on our rates or emails?

No. Emails are read through Anthropic's commercial API, whose terms exclude training on the data, and Laneworks never uses your rates for anyone else.

Can it lodge customs declarations?

No. Laneworks prices quotes only. Classification, valuation and declarations stay with your licensed staff and your customs system.
