When Nexcade is the better fit

If you want one set of AI agents across quoting, tenders, bookings and reporting, Nexcade covers more of the workflow. Laneworks doesn't do bookings or reporting.

When Laneworks is the better fit

  • You need every figure traceable. Prices come from your rate sheet by fixed rules, so a pricing manager can check any line back to its source row.
  • Your rates live in a spreadsheet. Send the rate sheet you already keep. No platform rollout.
  • You want quotes out fast without someone pressing send. Fully priced requests can go to the customer automatically; new lanes, expired rates, DG, reefer and oversize come to your team with the partner rate request written.
  • You want to see the price first. Plans are on the pricing page.

Questions

What is the difference between Nexcade and Laneworks?

Nexcade describes itself as AI agents that run freight quoting, tenders, rates, bookings and reporting with your team. Laneworks focuses on quoting: AI reads the request, and every price comes from your own rate sheet by fixed rules, so no figure is generated by AI.

Does Laneworks publish its pricing?

Yes. Plans start at A$1,500 a month for up to 150 quote requests, with unlimited users and no per-quote fees, on the pricing page. Nexcade's website asks you to request a demo.

Can Laneworks handle tenders?

Yes, priced per tender through Tender Desk, from your own rates, with anything not on your sheet flagged.

Is Laneworks affiliated with Nexcade?
