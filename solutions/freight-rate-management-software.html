Rate management software for freight forwarders

For a forwarder, rate management is everything between a carrier changing its price and your customer seeing a number. It covers five things:

  • Buy rates. What carriers, co-loaders, airlines, hauliers and overseas agents charge you, by lane, container type or weight break.
  • Sell rates. What you charge your customer: the buy rate plus your margin, or a sell tariff you keep directly. Many forwarders also hold different margins for different customers.
  • Validity dates. Every rate has a valid-from and valid-to date. Ocean rates and surcharges move often, so a rate that was right last month can lose you money today.
  • Surcharges. Bunker, peak season, general rate increases and other carrier surcharges that sit on top of the base freight.
  • Local charges. Origin and destination terminal handling, documentation, pickup and delivery, customs clearance. These depend on the port, the container and the Incoterm, and they are where quotes most often go wrong. Our Local Charges Index tracks what import containers pay at AU and UK terminals.

Most forwarders keep all of this in Excel, an email folder and a few people's heads. Storing the rates is the easy part. The hard part is using the right one, on every line, on every quote, while the customer is waiting. That is what freight rate management software should fix.

What is freight rate management software?

Freight rate management software holds a forwarder's buy rates, sell rates, validity dates, surcharges and local charges in one place, keeps them current when carriers and agents change them, and makes sure the right rate is used on every quote.

Do we have to rebuild our rate sheet?

No. Send whatever you have; we map it onto our template and check it before it goes live.

Can we have different margins per customer?

Yes. An optional customer markups sheet adds a percentage per customer or domain, shown in the price breakdown.

What about rates that start next month?

They're added with their start date, and today's rate keeps being used until then.

What happens when a lane isn't on our sheet or the rate has expired?

The line is flagged on the draft instead of being guessed, and the rate request to your partner or agent is written for that leg, ready to send.
