How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.

Alongside Magaya, not instead of it

  • Nothing to install and no access to your Magaya data: it works from the emails you forward.
  • Your shipments, bookings and accounting stay in Magaya exactly as they are today.
  • Each draft carries a quote reference, so a booking can be matched to the quote it came from.
  • USD freight and local charges in other currencies, with a total for each.

When rates change

Forward the carrier's rate notice or your new sheet to your rates address (quotes+yourco-rates@getlaneworks.com), in the email or attached as Excel, CSV, PDF or Word. Changed rates are merged in minutes, rates with a later start date are added from that date, nothing else is touched, and you get one email listing every change. Reply "undo" to roll it back.

Questions

Do we need to change anything in Magaya?

No. Laneworks works from email, so Magaya stays exactly as it is.

Where do the rates come from?

From your own rate sheet, which we load from whatever format you have. The AI reads the request; it never sets a price.

Is it a replacement for Magaya's own quoting?

No. It's for the quote requests that arrive by email and get answered by hand today.
