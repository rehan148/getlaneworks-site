How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.

Questions

Does it work with CargoWise?

Yes. It works by email beside CargoWise and never connects to it. When the customer books, the job goes into CargoWise the way it does today.

Which charges can it include?

Any line in your rate sheet: ocean or air freight, surcharges, terminal and documentation fees, cartage by delivery zone, clearance and biosecurity. If a lane or charge is missing, the draft says so instead of guessing.

How long does setup take?

Send your rate sheet and name who should receive drafts. Most forwarders are live the same day.
