How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • You check and send the drafted reply from your own mailbox. Laneworks never emails your customers.

Built for North American quoting

  • Ocean into LA/Long Beach, New York/New Jersey, Savannah, Houston, Vancouver and Montreal; air into the main gateways.
  • Your destination charges, customs brokerage, ISF and drayage lines exactly as your rate sheet sets them. ZIP and postal codes are read from the email.
  • Quotes in USD or CAD, or whatever your rate sheet uses.
  • Works by email alongside CargoWise, Magaya or any other system. Nothing to install.

Questions

Do you work with US and Canadian forwarders?

No. It quotes your ISF and brokerage fees as lines from your rate sheet; your team files as today.

Yes, if your rate sheet has drayage by ZIP or zone. If a ZIP isn't covered, the draft flags it instead of guessing.
