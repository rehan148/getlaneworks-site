How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • You check and send the drafted reply from your own mailbox. Laneworks never emails your customers.

Built for NVOCC rate requests

  • FCL by 20GP, 40GP and 40HC, and LCL per W/M with your minimums, from your own contract and tariff rates.
  • Origin and destination charges applied by Incoterm, so EXW, FOB and CIF requests each get the right lines.
  • USD ocean freight and local charges in your own currency, with a total for each.
  • Rates with validity dates: next month's rates are used only from their start date.
  • Works by email alongside CargoWise, Magaya or any other system. Nothing to install.

When rates change

Forward the carrier's rate notice or your new sheet to your rates address (quotes+yourco-rates@getlaneworks.com), in the email or attached as Excel, CSV, PDF or Word. Changed rates are merged in minutes, rates with a later start date are added from that date, nothing else is touched, and you get one email listing every change. Reply "undo" to roll it back.

Questions

Does it publish or file our tariff?

No. It reads your rate sheet to price quotes. Tariff publication stays with your existing process.

Can it handle surcharges like BAF, PSS and GRI?

Yes. Add them to your rate sheet as charges per container, or include them in an all-in rate; each one shows as its own line on the draft.

What if a lane isn't in our rates?

The draft says which lane or charge is missing instead of guessing, so your team can add it once and it prices automatically from then on.
