ChatGPT for freight forwarders: can it price your quotes?

ChatGPT for freight forwarders is good at the reading and writing around a quote: pulling the origin, destination, cargo and Incoterm out of a messy email, and drafting a polite reply. It is not built to set the price. It doesn't hold your current rates, it can get arithmetic and surcharges wrong without saying so, and it can't show which rate row a number came from. What ChatGPT does well for a forwarder

  • Reading a quote request written in shorthand or broken English and listing the shipment details.
  • Drafting or polishing a reply, a follow-up or a rate-increase notice.
  • Explaining an Incoterm, a surcharge or a chargeable-weight calculation to a new team member.
  • Summarising a long email chain before you pick it up.

Where it goes wrong on pricing

  • It doesn't know your rates. Unless you paste your tariff into every chat, any freight figure it gives is a guess from general knowledge, not your buy rate plus your margin.
  • Pasted rate sheets drift. Rates change weekly. A sheet pasted into a chat last month is now out of date, and nothing tells you.
  • Arithmetic and rules are unreliable. W/M for LCL, minimums, chargeable weight for air, charges that depend on the Incoterm: a general assistant can apply them inconsistently and still sound confident.
  • No audit trail. When a customer queries a line, there's no rate row to point to, so someone re-prices the quote by hand anyway.
  • Customer data. Check your plan's settings before pasting customer emails and rates into a general chatbot.

Questions

Can ChatGPT give an accurate freight quote?

Only if it has your current rates and applies every rule correctly, and it can't show you where each figure came from. Use it to read and draft; take prices from your rate sheet.

Is it safe to paste customer emails into ChatGPT?

It depends on your plan and settings. Business plans generally exclude your data from model training; consumer accounts may not unless you turn it off. Laneworks uses quote emails only to price them, never to train AI models, and deletes stored copies after 30 days.

Can we build our own quoting bot with ChatGPT?

You can, but the hard part isn't the AI: it's rate lookup, Incoterm charges, minimums, currencies, keeping the sheet current and flagging what isn't covered. That is the part Laneworks has already built and tested.

Does Laneworks replace our pricing team?

No. It drafts the routine quotes so your team checks and sends instead of building each one, and it hands them the unusual ones with the reason flagged.
