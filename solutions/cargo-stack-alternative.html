When Cargo-Stack is the better fit

Cargo-Stack builds for Hong Kong and Greater Bay Area forwarders. If your customers write on WhatsApp in Cantonese and expect the reply in Chinese, it is built for that. It also fits if you want quotes, tenders, ULD load planning, booking and house documents on one record, and a market check against published indices before a price goes out. Laneworks does none of those after-quote steps.

When Laneworks is the better fit

  • Your requests arrive by email. Forward them from the inbox you already use, and replies go out from your own mailbox. There is no other workspace for your team to open.
  • You want replies sent for you. Switch on automatic replies and fully priced quotes go straight to your customer, usually within two minutes, day or night. Anything unusual still comes to your team first.
  • Door-to-door quotes need partner rates. Any leg not on your sheet is flagged and the rate request to your partner or agent is written for you.
  • Road and door delivery matter. Pallet, container cartage and door delivery priced by postcode zone from your own road rates.
  • You want the price up front. Plans are on the pricing page, with unlimited users and no per-quote fees.

Questions

What is the main difference between Cargo-Stack and Laneworks?

Cargo-Stack is a workspace for freight forwarders that covers quoting, tenders, load planning, booking and house documents, with more of the shipment file in build. Laneworks does one job: it prices every quote request from the rate sheet you already keep, by fixed rules, and replies in your name from your own mailbox.

Can Laneworks reply in Chinese?

Laneworks reads requests written in other languages, including Chinese, and drafts the reply in English. Cargo-Stack says it replies in English, Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese, in the language the customer wrote in.

Can Laneworks send quotes without a person checking them?

Yes, when you switch it on. Fully priced quotes with nothing flagged go to your customer from your own mailbox. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft. Cargo-Stack says a person approves every quotation.

Does Laneworks publish its pricing?

Yes. Plans start at A$1,500 a month for up to 150 quote requests, with unlimited users and no per-quote fees. We found no prices on cargo-stack.com.

Is Laneworks affiliated with Cargo-Stack?

No. Cargo-Stack is a trademark of its owner. This page compares information Cargo-Stack publishes on cargo-stack.com, checked 19 November 2026.
