What comes back

  • The shipper's own workbook: their columns, rows and layout untouched, with freight, origin charges, destination charges, total, currency and validity added to every lane.
  • The source of every price: each row names the rate-sheet row it came from, so your pricing team can check any lane in seconds.
  • Lanes your rates don't cover: marked "Rate needed" with the reason, never estimated.
  • The agent rate request: one email listing the missing lanes with volumes and exactly what to send back.
  • A summary: lanes priced, lanes waiting on a rate, and the annual value of the priced lanes at the shipper's stated volumes.

Nothing is guessed

Questions

How long does a tender take?

Usually the same afternoon for a few hundred lanes, once your rates are loaded. The lanes your rates don't cover wait only on your agents' replies.

What format does the tender need to be in?

The shipper's own Excel workbook, as received. Laneworks finds the header row and the origin, destination, equipment and volume columns; a sheet it can't read is reported, not guessed at.

A$950 per tender up to 300 lanes, plus GST, by card or invoice. No subscription and no minimum: you pay only for tenders you send.

Does it work with CargoWise?
