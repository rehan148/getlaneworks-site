Replies that send themselves

Switch on auto-reply and a request that is fully priced from your rates, with nothing flagged, goes straight back to the customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, nights and weekends included. A missing rate, a guessed detail or a quote above the limit you set comes to your team as a draft instead, and you get a copy of every reply sent.

What stays with your team

  • A lane or charge missing from your rate sheet: the draft names it instead of guessing.
  • Details that had to be assumed, like an Incoterm the customer didn't state.
  • Quotes above the limit you set, lanes you want to price by hand, and requests from your own staff.
  • Vague requests ("rate for a box?") get the right questions back, and you choose whether those go automatically too.

How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.

Questions

Can we turn it off?

Any time, by email. Everything then comes to your team as a ready-to-send draft.

Prices come from your sheet by fixed rules, so a wrong price means a wrong rate in the sheet. Fix it once and every later quote is right.
