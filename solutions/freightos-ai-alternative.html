What Freightos announced

On 10 November 2026 Freightos announced the next generation of Freightos for Forwarders, for air and ocean. In its own words, the platform brings rates, quoting, booking and market intelligence into one connected environment, with AI agents built into the daily work. Trade press has called the AI side Freightos AI. The main points Freightos publishes:

  • Requests to quotes. Embedded AI turns unstructured customer requests from different channels into quotes, applying client-specific SOPs and margins.
  • Carrier connectivity. Connectors to more than 75 global air and ocean carriers, plus the 7LFreight trucking network, alongside your own contract rates.
  • Market checks. Live carrier capacity and Freightos Terminal benchmarks show how a price compares with the market.
  • Open APIs. Freightos rates, carrier connections and workflows can plug into your TMS or ERP.
  • Availability. Live globally for international air and ocean forwarders. Freightos invites forwarders to book a demonstration.

When Freightos is the better fit

Freightos fits if you want buy rates, carrier connectivity, booking and market benchmarks in one platform, especially for international air and ocean. If your team already uses WebCargo to buy air capacity, or you want an API layer to feed your own systems, it is built for that. Laneworks does not buy rates, connect to carriers or book freight.

When Laneworks is the better fit

  • Your requests arrive by email. Forward them from the inbox you already use, and replies go out from your own mailbox. There is no new platform for your team to work in.
  • Your sell rates live in a spreadsheet. Laneworks prices from that sheet. Rate updates arrive by email too: forward a carrier notice to your rates address, and you get one email listing every change. Reply "undo" and the previous sheet comes back.
  • You want replies sent for you. Switch on automatic replies and fully priced quotes go straight to your customer, usually within two minutes, day or night. A missing rate, a guessed detail or a total above your limit comes to your team first.
  • Door-to-door quotes need partner rates. Any leg not on your sheet is flagged and the rate request to your partner or agent is written for you.
  • You want the price up front. Plans are on the pricing page, with unlimited users and no per-quote fees.

Questions

What is the main difference between Freightos for Forwarders and Laneworks?

Freightos for Forwarders is a platform that brings rates, quoting, carrier connectivity, booking and market data together, with AI agents inside it. Laneworks does one job: it prices every quote request from the rate sheet you already keep, by fixed rules, and replies in your name from your own mailbox.

Does Laneworks connect to carriers or book freight?

No. Laneworks quotes from your own sell rates. Buying rates and booking stay with your carriers, platforms and TMS. Freightos says its platform links more than 75 air and ocean carriers and supports booking.

Can Laneworks send quotes without a person checking them?

Yes, when you switch it on. Fully priced quotes with nothing flagged go to your customer from your own mailbox, under a total limit you set. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.

What happens when a rate is missing or expired?

The quote is held and the lane is named. Laneworks never guesses a price. It writes the rate request to your partner or agent, and when their reply is forwarded to your rates address, the waiting quote is priced again.
