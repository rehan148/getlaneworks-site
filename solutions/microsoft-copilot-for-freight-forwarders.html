What Copilot in Outlook does well

  • Summarising a long customer thread before you reply.
  • Drafting a reply or follow-up in your usual tone.
  • Finding the last quote you sent a customer.
  • Tidying up wording in a rate notice or a tender response.

What it doesn't do for a quote

  • Look up the lane, container and validity on your rate sheet.
  • Add origin and destination charges according to the Incoterm.
  • Work out W/M for LCL, minimums and chargeable weight for air the same way every time.
  • Price door delivery by postcode or zone from your cartage table.
  • Flag a missing lane, reefer or dangerous-goods rate instead of writing a plausible figure.

You can build some of this yourself with Copilot Studio, but you then own the rate lookup, the charge rules and keeping the sheet current.

How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.
