How to get yours, free

  1. Export last month's quotes from CargoWise, your quoting tool or your spreadsheet: one row per quote with origin, destination, container or weight, and the total quoted. 50 is plenty; send more if you like.
  2. Attach the rate sheet your team quoted from, as it is. Excel, CSV or PDF.
  3. Email both to rehan@getlaneworks.com. Within one working day you get the same workbook back with five columns added, plus a one-page summary of the shortfall.

Your quotes and rates are used only to run the check, never to train AI models, and are deleted within 30 days after you stop using Laneworks, or sooner if you ask.

What it catches

  • Below cost: quotes that didn't even cover the rates on your own sheet.
  • Below your markup: quotes that covered cost but not the margin your sheet says to charge.
  • Charges left off: where the gap equals one charge on your sheet, the note names it.
  • Lanes your sheet doesn't cover: marked "Not checked" with the reason, never estimated. These are also the lanes your next rate request should ask for.

Nothing is guessed

Each quote is re-priced by the same fixed rules Laneworks uses to price live quote requests: port, container size, rate validity on the quote date, every local charge on your sheet, your markup. No AI sets a number. How a Laneworks price is set

Questions

Is the margin check really free?

Yes. No card, no contract, no call needed. You get the checked workbook and the summary either way.

What format do the quotes need to be in?

Any export with one row per quote: origin, destination, container type or weight and volume, and the amount quoted. Laneworks finds the header row and the columns itself; a sheet it can't read is reported, not guessed at.

What if our quotes are in different currencies?

Quotes in the rate sheet's currency are checked. Others are marked 'Not checked' with the reason, so nothing is converted at a guessed rate.

Who sees our data?

Only the Laneworks team running the check. It is never used to train AI models and is deleted within 30 days after you stop using Laneworks, or sooner if you ask.

Does it work with CargoWise?

Yes. A quote export from CargoWise, or any spreadsheet your team keeps, works as it is.
