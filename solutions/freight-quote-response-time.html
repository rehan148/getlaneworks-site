How to cut freight quote response time

Freight quote response time is mostly waiting, not working: the request sits in a shared inbox until someone has twenty minutes to find the rates, add the charges and write the reply. Shippers often send the same request to several forwarders, and the first complete, believable price is often the one that gets the booking. Here's where the time goes and how to take it out.

Where the time goes

  • Waiting in the inbox: requests that arrive during a busy morning, after hours or at the weekend wait for a person.
  • Reading the request: pulling lane, cargo, weight, dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address out of an email and a packing list.
  • Finding the rates: the right sheet, the right validity, the right container size.
  • Adding everything else: origin and destination charges, clearance, delivery zone, markup, currency.
  • Writing it up: a reply the customer can understand and book from.

Five ways to reply faster

  • Keep one rate sheet per mode with a 'valid to' date on every rate, so nobody checks whether a rate is current.
  • Price local charges once, as a table, instead of re-typing them into every quote.
  • Use a standard reply format customers can book from: lines, totals, validity, what's excluded.
  • Ask for the missing details in the first reply, priced on stated assumptions, instead of a question with no price.
  • Automate the routine requests and keep people for the unusual ones. That's what Laneworks does.

Questions

What is a good freight quote response time?

Same business day is the norm for routine FCL, LCL and air requests; faster wins more of them. When a shipper sends one request to several forwarders, a complete price in minutes is hard to beat. Laneworks replies to routine requests in about two minutes, any time of day.

Why are freight quotes slow?

Because each one is manual: reading the request, finding the current rate, adding origin, destination and clearance charges, then writing it up. Each step is quick; together, and behind a busy inbox, they take hours.
