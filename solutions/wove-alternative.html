When Wove is the better fit

If you run everything in CargoWise and want one platform for quotes, shipment registration, customs entries and carrier invoice checks, Wove covers more ground. Laneworks doesn't do customs or AP.

When Laneworks is the better fit

  • You want quotes out without someone pressing send. Fully priced requests go to your customer in your name, at any hour. Anything not on your sheet (new lane, expired rate, reefer, DG, oversize) comes to your team as a draft instead.
  • Your rates live in a spreadsheet. Send the rate sheet you already keep. Every line on the quote names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Door-to-door quotes need partner rates. Laneworks writes the rate request for each missing leg, so your team isn't retyping the job to three agents.
  • You want to see the price first. Plans are on the pricing page, with unlimited users and no per-quote fees.

How it works

  • Forward the customer's request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address, from the email and any packing list or scanned PDF.
  • We price it from your rate sheet and attach a PDF quotation in your company's name.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. Your choice, per customer.

Questions

What is the main difference between Wove and Laneworks for quoting?

Wove says its replies are drafted for your team to approve. Laneworks drafts too, and when you switch it on it also sends fully priced quotes in your name, with anything unusual held back for your team.

Do I need CargoWise to use Laneworks?

No. Laneworks prices from the rate sheet you already keep, in Excel or another format, and works from email. It suits forwarders on CargoWise and those who aren't.

Does Laneworks do customs declarations or AP invoices?

No. Laneworks does quoting in depth: reading requests, pricing, partner rate requests, PDF quotations and sending. Wove covers customs entries and AP invoice reconciliation as well.
