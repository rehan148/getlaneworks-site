Where the two differ

  • Scope. Helm is an AI inbox that covers more than quoting. Laneworks focuses on one task: priced quote replies from the customer's email.
  • Pricing. Helm is published at US$1,000 a month for 1 to 10 connected mailboxes with unlimited users (its pricing page, checked October 2026). Laneworks is priced in AUD by monthly quote volume, from A$1,500 a month including 150 quote requests.
  • Connection. Helm connects to your mailboxes. Laneworks needs no access to your mailbox or TMS: you forward the emails you want drafted, by hand or with a forwarding rule.
  • Pricing method. Laneworks prices by fixed rules against your rate sheet and names the row every line came from, so nothing is AI-guessed.

If you want an AI assistant across the whole inbox, Helm is worth a look. If quoting is the job you want fixed, from your own rates, with no connection to your mail system, Laneworks is the lighter option. See the 2026 comparison.

How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.
