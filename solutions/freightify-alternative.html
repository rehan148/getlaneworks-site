Where the two differ

  • What it is. Freightify is a rate management platform with carrier rate search, quoting and an Outlook add-in. Laneworks does one job: it reads the customer's quote-request email and drafts the reply from your own rate sheet.
  • Whose rates. Freightify is built around managing and searching rates in its platform. Laneworks uses the rate sheet you already keep, sent as a spreadsheet.
  • Pricing. Freightify pricing is on request, with seat and volume caps and 6- or 12-month billing, with a 7-day trial (as published on its pricing page, checked October 2026). Laneworks is a flat monthly fee per quoting mailbox, cancel any time, with a free trial on your own emails.
  • Setup. A platform rollout versus sending a rate sheet and forwarding an email the same day.

Freightify is the stronger choice if rate procurement and a quoting portal are what you need. Laneworks fits if your quotes start as emails and your rates live in a spreadsheet. See the full 2026 comparison.

How it works

  • Forward the customer's quote request to your Laneworks address, attachments and all.
  • We read it: lane, cargo, weights and dimensions, Incoterm and delivery address are pulled out of the email and any packing list.
  • We price it by fixed rules against your own rate sheet. The AI never sets a price, and every line names the rate-sheet row it came from.
  • Sent for you, or checked by you. With auto-reply on, fully priced quotes go straight to your customer from your own mailbox, usually within two minutes, 24/7. Anything unusual comes to your team as a draft.

Questions

Can I use Laneworks alongside Freightify?

Yes. Laneworks works from email and doesn't connect to other systems, so it can sit beside any platform you already use.

Does Laneworks search carrier rates?

No. It prices from your own rate sheet. If you need live carrier rate search, that's a rate-management platform's job.
